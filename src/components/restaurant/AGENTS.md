# Restaurant & Bar AGENTS.md

## Purpose

The restaurant and bar side of the app: the till, the guest tab a stay can charge to, walk-in tabs with no room behind them, settling at the counter, and the running tab a guest can ask for mid-meal.

## Ownership

- Primary Owner: Frontend Engineers / Antigravity Agent
- Scope: `src/components/restaurant/**`, `src/components/RestaurantTab.tsx`, and the tab data layer (`src/utils/tabs.ts`, `src/utils/tabRevenue.ts`, `src/utils/restaurantMenu.ts`, `src/utils/restaurantFocus.ts`).

## Local Contracts

    - [restaurantMenu.ts](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/utils/restaurantMenu.ts) — the restaurant and bar menu (k70). **The database is the source of truth**: `getMenu` reads `menu_categories` + `menu_items` (both async) and `saveMenu` upserts the whole list for every device, seeded from the hotel's own printed card — **61 items in 15 groups** (Breakfast 6–10am, Value Meals, Rice, Appetizers, Chicken, Pork, Beef, Seafood, Noodles, Vegetables & Salad, Snacks, Desserts & Shakes, Drinks, Beer & Spirits). Each item carries a stable readable id, a name, a price and an optional `note` for the cooking choices a dish comes with (Shrimp *Buttered | Sinagang | Tinola*). `DEFAULT_MENU` is kept as the offline fallback — the same list typed into the code — so the till still works with the database unreachable.


    - [tabs.ts](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/utils/tabs.ts) — the guest tab (board card k69), typed by [types/tab.ts](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/types/tab.ts): `openTab` (hands back the booking's existing open tab instead of opening a second), `getOpenTabForBooking`, `getOpenTabs` (**every** open tab in one read — a stay's and a diner's alike, which is what the Restaurant screen's strip is built from; the diner tabs are the ones with no `booking_id`), `getOpenTabLinesByBooking` (the lines for several bookings at once, for a bill covering a group), `closeTab`, `settleTab` (takes the money for a tab, gives it a numbered receipt and closes it), `getTabLines`, `getAllTabLines` (every line on every tab, read once for the Earnings Report), `addTabLine`, `deleteTabLine`, and `tabTotal`. Supabase primary + localStorage fallback, same as inventory and cleaning. It writes **only** `tabs`/`tab_lines` — never a booking row, which is precisely what keeps the booking RPCs out of the tab feature.


    - [tabRevenue.ts](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/utils/tabRevenue.ts) — `foodMoneyIn(lines, start, end)`: the food and bar money ordered in a period, read from the tab lines (k69, part E), returned as a total plus a day-by-day map so the Earnings Report can show the figure and the income trend can plot it on the day each order was placed. The day comes from the line's own timestamp read as a **local** date (`dateToString`): an order taken at 7am in UTC+8 is 23:00 the previous day in UTC, and reading it as UTC would file a whole breakfast shift under yesterday.


  - [RestaurantTab](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/components/RestaurantTab.tsx) — the `/restaurant` tab, **the till for both kinds of guest** (k69 part B + k70), in the shape the owner picked from the three drawn proposals ('P2', .lavish/restaurant-redesign.html). ## Work Guidance

- **Guest Tab (k69) — the running food and bar bill**: A stay can run up food and bar charges that settle at check-out. The lines live in `tabs`/`tab_lines` (`utils/tabs.ts`), **never on the booking**, because a food line is a *small* save and a partial booking save would blank the fields `update_booking` copies with no fallback (`payment_method`, `payment_reference`, `payment_plan`, `notes`, `prepared_by`, the guest contact/address fields, `company_name`, `vehicle_plate`, `invoice_type`). The rules, all decided by the owner: **anyone can run a tab**, walk-in diners with no room included (the restaurant is most of this trade) — so a tab may exist with no `booking_id`; **one tab per booking row**; **food can only be added once the guest is checked in** — the owner's rule, set after testing: people order when they are physically in the hotel, never over the phone, so a booked-but-not-arrived stay has a **read-only** tab showing *Check the guest in first* (the walk-in restaurant tab has no such gate, because that diner is standing there); the tab total joins the bill, so `balance_due` reads *stay + tab − paid*; the **deposit stays stay-only**, which is why `amountToPayNow(booking, tabTotal)` takes the tab out before working out the 50% — a lunch eaten after booking must never inflate what the desk asks on arrival; a wrong line is **simply removed** — the owner changed this from the first plan, so the tab now follows the very same rule as a wrong payment: take the line off and the bill recomputes itself, one money rule instead of two (the old `correction` columns stay in the table but nothing writes them); and the guest can ask for a **running tab printout** mid-stay, not only a bill at check-out. **Orders are taken in the Restaurant screen, for both kinds of guest** — the owner's correction after reviewing the first build: a menu board squeezed into the narrow booking slide-over was the wrong place for it, so the booking's Guest tab now shows the food, the running total, the bin and the print button and hands staff over (`ordering={false}` + `onOpenTill`), and the Restaurant screen charges a room by picking the guest out of the **strip across the top** (`Being served` in `RestaurantTab`), which lists the diner tabs and every guest who is in the hotel, each with what they owe so far. The bill and the writing are **shared, never copied**: `TabBill` draws the lines, the total, the bin and the print button on both screens, and `useTabOrder` is the only place a line is added or removed. Built so far: the two tables, `utils/tabs.ts`, `GuestTabPanel` (add a charge, remove a line, running total), the money wiring — `utils/bookingBalance.ts` folds the tab into the stored `balance_due`, `amountToPayNow(booking, tabTotal)` keeps the deposit stay-only — the printed bill, which reads the tab and prints the food lines under the room, and the `/restaurant` tab where a **walk-in diner's** tab is opened by name or table, and **settling at the counter** (`settleTab` + `TabSettlePanel` + `TabReceiptModal`): the diner pays the tab's own total, the payment is recorded with its own numbered receipt, and the tab closes.
