# Restaurant & Bar AGENTS.md

## Purpose

The restaurant and bar side of the app: the till, the guest tab a stay can charge to, walk-in tabs with no room behind them, settling at the counter, and the running tab a guest can ask for mid-meal.

## Ownership

- Primary Owner: Frontend Engineers / Antigravity Agent
- Scope: `src/components/restaurant/**`, `src/components/RestaurantTab.tsx`, and the tab data layer (`src/utils/tabs.ts`, `src/utils/tabRevenue.ts`, `src/utils/restaurantMenu.ts`, `src/utils/restaurantFocus.ts`).

## Work Guidance

- **Guest Tab (k69) — the running food and bar bill**: A stay can run up food and bar charges that settle at check-out. The lines live in `tabs`/`tab_lines` (`utils/tabs.ts`), **never on the booking**, because a food line is a *small* save and a partial booking save would blank the fields `update_booking` copies with no fallback (`payment_method`, `payment_reference`, `payment_plan`, `notes`, `prepared_by`, the guest contact/address fields, `company_name`, `vehicle_plate`, `invoice_type`). The rules, all decided by the owner: **anyone can run a tab**, walk-in diners with no room included (the restaurant is most of this trade) — so a tab may exist with no `booking_id`; **one tab per booking row**; **food can only be added once the guest is checked in** — the owner's rule, set after testing: people order when they are physically in the hotel, never over the phone, so a booked-but-not-arrived stay has a **read-only** tab showing *Check the guest in first* (the walk-in restaurant tab has no such gate, because that diner is standing there); the tab total joins the bill, so `balance_due` reads *stay + tab − paid*; the **deposit stays stay-only**, which is why `amountToPayNow(booking, tabTotal)` takes the tab out before working out the 50% — a lunch eaten after booking must never inflate what the desk asks on arrival; a wrong line is **simply removed** — the owner changed this from the first plan, so the tab now follows the very same rule as a wrong payment: take the line off and the bill recomputes itself, one money rule instead of two (the old `correction` columns stay in the table but nothing writes them); and the guest can ask for a **running tab printout** mid-stay, not only a bill at check-out. **Orders are taken in the Restaurant screen, for both kinds of guest** — the owner's correction after reviewing the first build: a menu board squeezed into the narrow booking slide-over was the wrong place for it, so the booking's Guest tab now shows the food, the running total, the bin and the print button and hands staff over (`ordering={false}` + `onOpenTill`), and the Restaurant screen charges a room by picking the guest out of the list of people who are actually in the hotel. Built so far: the two tables, `utils/tabs.ts`, `GuestTabPanel` (add a charge, remove a line, running total), the money wiring — `utils/bookingBalance.ts` folds the tab into the stored `balance_due`, `amountToPayNow(booking, tabTotal)` keeps the deposit stay-only — the printed bill, which reads the tab and prints the food lines under the room, and the `/restaurant` tab where a **walk-in diner's** tab is opened by name or table, and **settling at the counter** (`settleTab` + `TabSettlePanel` + `TabReceiptModal`): the diner pays the tab's own total, the payment is recorded with its own numbered receipt, and the tab closes. Still to come: nothing — the mid-stay printout (part D, `RunningTabSlip`) and the earnings-report line (part E, the *Restaurant & bar* row) are both built.

- **Restaurant Menu Board (k70)**: Orders are taken from the menu, never typed. `utils/restaurantMenu.ts` holds the list (`getMenu`/`saveMenu`, database-backed, transcribed from the hotel's laminated menu — **61 items in 15 groups**), and `components/restaurant/MenuPicker.tsx` is the till: category buttons, dish tiles, **one tap = one line on the tab** with the price filled in. It sits at the top of `GuestTabPanel`, so a stay's tab and a walk-in's use the same board, and **it is the whole order screen** — the typed row is hidden behind a *"+ Something not on the menu"* link, so the first thing staff see is the menu, never a blank box. **It is a staff tool, not a guest screen** — nobody hands a tablet to a diner — so it is judged on how fast a hand finds a dish: icons for the food groups, the price as the loudest element, big targets, and a count badge on anything already ordered. Dishes that come with a cooking choice carry it as a `note` (shown on the tile's tooltip); proper modifier buttons are a follow-up (card k109). **The menu lives in the database** (`menu_categories` + `menu_items`, seeded with all 61 items), so every tablet shows the same menu and the same prices; `DEFAULT_MENU` is only the offline fallback. Editing the menu still needs a screen (card k105).
