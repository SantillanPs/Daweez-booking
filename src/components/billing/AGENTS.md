The only deliberate blanks remaining are the printed fill-in lines staff or the guest complete by hand: **Prepared by** (prints its rule so the name can be written in) and **Guest Signature**. There is no unit key/legend at the foot of the statement. The **payment method is the guest's choice, not a form field** (card k132): it is recorded with the payment that actually arrives, so a statement printed at booking time names no method at all, and the every-way-to-pay list is the defensive fallback for bookings saved before this rule. **The statement prints only what applies** (owner's rule: never show a line that has nothing to say): only the **chosen** payment method is shown (never all four tick-boxes); **Account Details shows only the matching account** — the GCash name/number for GCash, the bank name/account for Bank Transfer, and nothing at all for cash — zero-value deduction rows (`Less: Downpayment/Deposit`, `Partial Payment`, `Other`) are hidden entirely, and the receptionist's `prepared_by` name is printed on the **Prepared by** line. **The bill answers the guest's own payment choice**: it prints the agreed **Payment Plan** (`Deposit (50%)` / `Full payment` / **`Custom · ₱1,000 now`** — the custom figure travels with the word, because `Custom` alone says nothing to the guest) beside the method, and **Amount Due** is what the guest hands over NOW — the agreed figure for a deposit or a custom plan, the whole stay for Full pay, labelled under the figure. **There is no `Balance on arrival` line** (removed on the owner's instruction): he read it as a second demand on the same page, and the Payment Plan label already says the figure is a deposit. Printing a bare `balance_due` there had made a deposit booking read as though the whole stay were due at once and contradicted what the guest chose. Match payment methods by whole word, not substring: `'gcash'.includes('cash')` is `true`, which previously ticked Cash on every GCash booking. Method matching and labels come from `utils/paymentMethod.ts` (`paymentKind` / `paymentMethodLabel`). The guest block prints **Sex** as well as Birth Date, Plate No., and the stay/companion details; a **Security Deposit** is printed as its own `Add:` row only when one is actually held.


  - [PaymentReceiptDocument](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/components/billing/PaymentReceiptDocument.tsx) + [PrintPaymentReceiptModal](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/components/billing/PrintPaymentReceiptModal.tsx) — the printable **Payment Receipt** given to a guest after each payment. Unlike the blank paper receipt it only shows what actually happened: the one payment method used (with its reference), the actual payment-for reason (Reservation / Partial Payment / Room Accommodation), and the real status (Paid in Full / Partially Paid / Booked Confirmed / Venue or Room Reserved). Wired into `ExtendStayModal` so recording a payment immediately offers a receipt and every stored payment can be reprinted. **Each receipt carries its own stored number** (`PaymentRecord.receipt_number`, e.g. `PR-202608-001`, assigned by `utils/receiptNumber.ts` when the payment is recorded) — never a number derived from the payment's position in the list, which used to renumber every later receipt as soon as one was removed. Payments recorded before numbering existed fall back to a derived number. "Previous Balance" reconstructs what the guest owed before this payment by splitting `downpayment_paid` into a booking-time deposit plus the recorded receipts, so legacy and imported bookings that paid without a stored receipt still show the right figure. The method shown is the payment's own method, matched by whole word via `utils/paymentMethod.ts`. **A short stay prints one extra line under `Room No`** — `Stay: Sep 25 · 3 hours` (the owner's S6 ruling): the slip the guest keeps has to prove what was bought, and `Room Accommodation` alone could be three hours or a night. Ordinary stays print nothing there. The day comes from `fmtDay` in `receiptText.ts`, which parses `YYYY-MM-DD` by hand because `new Date('2026-09-25')` is UTC midnight. **`Received From` and `Received By` are section headings, never fields**: a heading says what the labelled lines under it represent, so it carries no value of its own. Filling `Received From` with the guest's name printed that name twice (once there, once as the guest line) and told the reader nothing; the block now reads heading → `Guest:` / `Room No:` / `Status:`. **It prints on the hotel's 58 mm thermal roll**, so the slip is a **single column** (no side-by-side tables — a label and a long value cannot share one row at that width), **pure black on white** (the printer is 1-bit, so grey text only dithers into fuzz), 8px–17px type, and nothing wider than `w-[58mm]`; anything that can run long (guest name, invoice number, reference) gets its own `Block` line. The **screen preview is that same 58 mm slip**, so what staff see is what comes off the printer. `PrintPaymentReceiptModal` injects `@page { size: 58mm auto; margin: 3mm }` **only for the duration of the print** and removes it again, so the A4 Guest Billing Statement keeps its own page size.

- **Paper Guest Registration as Reference**: The staff-familiar paper Guest Registration form (Daweez Pension House) is the reference for the walk-in booking UI and the printed Guest Billing Statement — same fields (Birth Date, **Sex**, **Plate No.**, companion Name/Nationality) — except breakfast, which moved from each companion to each ROOM (card k140), payment methods (Cash/Gcash/Bank Transfer/Other), Pension Policies, standard check-in/out times, and a Guest Signature line.
