## Purpose

This directory contains the React + TypeScript frontend codebase for the Daweez Pension House property management system (PMS) staff dashboard and the guest-facing direct reservation portal.

## Money Rules

- **One price (card k128)**: there is one figure per room and one per venue — the rate card in Settings — with no sale mode, no crossed-out second price and no "promo" wording anywhere. Every booking records `promo_applied: true` because the single figure **is** the price, which is also what makes `calculatePricing` and the printed statement agree with the screen.
- **The money IS the status**: `unpaid` / `downpayment` / `paid` is derived from the payments recorded and is never typed. There is **no payment-status control anywhere** — list, quick view, calendar pill or details modal. To correct a mistake, remove the wrong payment; the balance and the badge recompute themselves.
- **The deposit is agreed, not computed behind the desk's back**: half the stay by default and typable; the agreed figure is stored in `agreed_deposit` and `amountToPayNow` is the one rule for what to ask for now (the deposit while nothing is recorded, the outstanding rest once a payment is in).
- **A walk-in booking starts unpaid** (`status: 'pending'`, shown as *Unpaid* / *On hold*): it still blocks the room, never expires, and **only recording money confirms it**. Partner/corporate bookings are contract-backed (`confirmed`). Editing a booking preserves its status.
- **The first payment confirms the booking**; every later one just moves the balance.
- **Write it down, never guess it**: an empty payment method is shown as `Choose…` and recording is refused until the desk picks one — defaulting to "Cash" is exactly how a GCash payment once printed a Cash receipt. The same rule applies in the booking form: the guest chooses the method, the desk records it, and nothing is preselected.
- **Never print a bare `balance_due` as "Balance on arrival"** — a deposit booking read as though the whole stay were due at once, and the guest saw two demands on one page.
- **Food follows check-in** (card k69): a stay charges food and bar to the room only once the guest is in; a diner with no room gets a walk-in tab settled at the counter; the food joins the bill at check-out. Details in the restaurant doc.
- **Breakfast is the ROOM's charge** (cards k140/k142): one charge for the whole stay at that room's own `breakfast_price`, chosen by the desk in the booking form — never per guest or per day — and folded into the room line on the bill. Details in the utils doc (`pricing.ts`) and the booking-form paragraph in `components/AGENTS.md`.

- **Dashboard Grid Layouts**: When creating 2-column split views (e.g. Form on the left, Data Ledger on the right), wrap the page title and description within the left column container. Do not span the title across the top of the grid, as this pushes the data column down and breaks vertical centering.

- **Settings Space Efficiency**: For repetitive configuration sections (like room settings or long URLs), prefer dense accordion lists over stacked padded cards. Use absolute-positioned action buttons (e.g. copy icons) embedded directly inside input fields to save horizontal space.

- **Readability & Badges Column Layout**: Do not apply restrictive max-width constraints to table columns that display badges or tags. Overrides and status indicators must render in clear, well-spaced badges using brand accent tokens (`brand-text`/`brand-bg`/`brand-border`, gold) for maximum readability.

- **Free-Typing Number Boxes**: Money/quantity inputs use the shared [NumInput](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/components/NumInput.tsx) (or a plain `type="text"` + `inputMode` box). Never use `type="number"` — the OS spinner and its "snap back to 0 on backspace" behavior confuse staff. NumInput types freely (Backspace clears it, can be empty) and still reports a clean number via `onChange`.

- **Money-First Payment Display**: Staff-facing payment info must read like plain language, never accounting columns. Use big, bold, color-coded labels — vibrant green for "Paid", amber for "Partial · ₱X left", strong red for "Owes ₱X" — via `utils/bookingMoney.ts`. Keep the single most important money number (e.g. "Who owes right now") visible at the top of money-related lists.

- **Always Apply UX Best Practices**: Every UI must be usable and space-smart by default — high information density, no wasted white space (never leave a dead row next to a small control), minimal scrolling (no "box on box" stacking), progressive disclosure (show rare/secondary options only when relevant; default to the common path rather than forcing a choice), multi-column/responsive grids to use horizontal width, clear labels/affordances/feedback, and accessibility. Prefer familiar patterns and keep it compact.

- **Report extras are configurable per group (Earnings Report)**: The report's **Extras** column shows a flat staff-editable amount per category group — **Rooms + Vacation House** share one (`RateConfig.accommodationExtras`), **Garden + Gazebo** share another (`RateConfig.venueExtras`), set in **Settings → Shared Rates**. These REPLACE the per-category extras figure (rooms no longer itemize booking extras in the summary; venues' "Included" becomes this amount) and are ADDED to each category's total. A category's Room Rate column stays the base, so Extras + base = Total. Per-room drill-down rows still show their real booking extras. **The Restaurant & bar row and column (k69, part E)** give the food and drinks a business line of their own — counted on the day each order was placed, from the tab lines (`utils/tabRevenue.ts`) rather than the bookings, so a lunch never reads as a room earning more — and that money is included in Total Money In and Your Profit, in the summary cards, in the donut and in the income trend.

- **Inline Required-Field Validation**: Booking forms validate required fields per field, not as one message at the bottom. Required boxes keep a red `*` label, get a red border (`border-danger-400`), and show a short red note right under the box when left empty — it fires live on blur and again on Save (mark empty fields with a touched/trySave flag). This keeps the note where the problem is instead of a single generic error.

- **Responsive Design**: Desktop-first layout. Top tab nav is the primary navigation. The UI is built for larger screens first with responsive adaptations for mobile. Bottom tab nav appears on mobile (`md:hidden`), while top tabs are always visible on desktop (`hidden md:block`). Tables use `overflow-x-auto` for horizontal scrolling on small screens. Calendar cells use responsive min-heights (`min-h-[80px] sm:min-h-[100px]`). On the `/calendar` route, the layout is viewport-locked (`h-screen overflow-hidden`) to eliminate dual scrollbars, keeping top controls and table headers stickily anchored while allowing the grid table itself to scroll horizontally and vertically.

- **Paper Guest Registration as Reference**: The staff-familiar paper Guest Registration form (Daweez Pension House) is the reference for the walk-in booking UI and the printed Guest Billing Statement — same fields (Birth Date, **Sex**, **Plate No.**, companion Name/Nationality) — except breakfast, which moved from each companion to each ROOM (card k140), payment methods (Cash/Gcash/Bank Transfer/Other), Pension Policies, standard check-in/out times, and a Guest Signature line. Keep the digital form faithful but simple so staff recognize it. Print a field only when the booking actually holds it (never a label with nothing under it).

- **One Payment, One Numbered Receipt**: Every payment — walk-in, in-stay, or taken online at booking — creates its own `PaymentRecord` carrying a **stored** `receipt_number` (`PR-YYYYMM-NNN`). Never derive a receipt number from the payment's index: removing one payment would renumber every later receipt, and a number staff quote to a guest must stay stable. Before adding a payment, check `methodNeedsReference` (`utils/paymentMethod.ts`) — GCash and bank need a reference, cash does not. **Every payment carries its OWN method.** The first payment uses the method agreed when the booking was made — printed on the card as fixed text, never asked again, since the booking form already captured it. From the **second** payment on, the quick view's **Pay by** picker appears so the guest can settle the rest another way, the record stores the method actually used, and the booking's `payment_method` follows the latest one. Never print one method for money that arrived two ways — the receipts each name their own.

- **Money reads come from the money recorded**: a receipt or statement takes its method, reference and Previous Balance from that payment's own `payment_records` entry — never from the method the guest merely agreed to at booking time (`paymentKind`/`paymentMethodLabel` in `utils/paymentMethod.ts`, `paymentBreakdown` in `utils/receiptNumber.ts`). The quick view does **not** summarise what has been received line by line: the **Payment receipts** block already itemises every payment with its amount, method, date and time, so the money card shows the two figures plus the progress bar, and nothing is said twice. **One input per value**: the money card owns the reference box for the payment about to be taken (it appears only for GCash/bank) and the single button below submits it, so the same number is never asked for twice.

- **Standard Check-in / Check-out Times**: Check-in is 2 PM and check-out is 12 PM by default. The Check in / Check out buttons compute early/late hours automatically against these (configurable in Settings→Rates as `standardCheckInTime` / `standardCheckOutTime`). Early/late is charged **only at the actual check-in/out** (rooms ₱/hr up to `lateEarlyCapHours`, then one full night; venues charge the venue hourly rate, e.g. ₱500/hr, hourly for any overlap incl. Gazebo & Garden) — never during booking, because the arrival/departure time isn't known yet.

- **Double Booking Prevention**: Any transaction or booking action must run collision checks through `isRoomAvailable` or `isVenueRangeAvailable`. Check-in dates must be chronologically earlier than check-out dates.

- **Staff Passcode Authentication Gate**: The staff portal is protected by a client-side passcode authentication gate. Authorized passcodes include 'daweez2026' (case-insensitive), '8888', and any custom passcode defined in VITE_STAFF_PASSCODE. Successful authentication persists in localStorage as `daweez_pms_auth` until explicit logout.

- **Performance Guardrails for Grids & Complex Views**:
  - **State Isolation**: High-frequency states (e.g. mouse hover coordinates, hovered dates, range calculations) must live at the lowest leaf component level possible. Avoid placing hover states in orchestrators or context providers to prevent wide component trees from re-rendering.
  - **Memoized Cell Wrappers**: Wrap individual grid cells or list items in a memoized component wrapper (`React.memo`) using strict custom equality comparators to bypass rendering. Ensure callbacks are stable or checked appropriately, converting $O(N)$ re-renders to $O(1)$ changes. A memo comparator MUST compare every prop that affects the rendered output or an onClick closure (e.g. the cell's `date`, `isoStr`, `id`), and key dynamic lists by stable data identity (e.g. the day's ISO string), NOT array index — otherwise a reused component instance keeps a stale value in its handler after data changes (this caused the timeline to sometimes save a wrong, month-shifted check-out date like Aug 20 when July 20 was clicked).
  - **Numeric Timestamp Operations**: Avoid using string parsing operations (like `.toDateString()` or `.toISOString()`) inside rendering loops. Convert dates to numeric timestamps via `.getTime()` for cheap integer comparisons.
  - **Keystroke Rendering Isolation**: Keep text field keystroke states isolated so typing in registry forms does not trigger re-calculations or re-renders of adjacent sidebar receipts and summaries.
  - **Avoid Backdrop Filter Penalty**: Do not apply `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` styling to modals or overlay backdrops rendered on top of dense grids or tables. This triggers heavy GPU composition paint operations on every keypress or state change. Use solid or semi-transparent flat colors instead (e.g. `bg-slate-900/50`).
  - **Debounced Hover Tooltips**: Details tooltips on dense timeline grids must be debounced with a short delay (e.g., `500ms`) using a `useRef` timeout to prevent rendering spam when moving the mouse across cells. Stale timeouts must be cleared on mouse leave and on component unmount.
  - **Floating UI Alerts over Inline Banners**: Transient states (such as active scheduler selection status) must be presented in absolute/fixed floating elements (toasts) rather than inline blocks to avoid grid layout shifts, vertical screen clutter, and component displacement.
  - **No Browser Dialogs — ever**: `alert()`, `confirm()` and `prompt()` block the entire app, steal focus out of the box staff were typing in, and never say which action raised them. Everything a popup used to say has a proper home: a **field** problem (missing amount, missing GCash reference) is shown **inline under that box**; a problem with a **button just pressed** (checking out while money is owed) is shown **inline under that button**; anything else transient ("Partner deal saved.", "Could not save the rates.") goes through **`showToast`**. **Destructive actions ask through `askConfirm`** (`utils/confirm.ts` → [ConfirmDialog](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez/src/components/ConfirmDialog.tsx)): deleting an expense, a category or a partner profile, removing a payment, cancelling a booking. The codebase contains **no native browser dialogs at all** — `alert`, `confirm` and `prompt` are all gone.
  - **Never pass a parameterised handler straight to `onClick`**: `onClick={onSubmit}` hands the DOM **click event** to `onSubmit` as its first argument. When that argument is an optional override — as in `handleAddReceipt(amountOverride?, thenCheckIn?)` — the event (a `MouseEvent`) silently becomes the amount, `Number(event)` is `NaN`, and a form that already showed ₱1,250 reported "enter a payment amount" and refused to save. Always wrap it: `onClick={() => onSubmit()}`, and keep the handler defensive by accepting an override only when `typeof it === 'number'`.
  - **Lazy-Route Dependency Pre-Bundling**: Dependencies reachable only through a lazy-loaded route (e.g. `@tanstack/charts` on `/analytics`) must be listed in `vite.config.ts` → `optimizeDeps.include`. Otherwise Vite discovers them on first navigation and answers `504 Outdated Optimize Dep`, which fails the dynamic import with `Failed to fetch dynamically imported module`.
