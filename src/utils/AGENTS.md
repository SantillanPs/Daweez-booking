# Utils AGENTS.md

## Purpose

The data and money layer under the whole app: database access, the pricing engine, the money helpers, receipts, rates and every stored configuration list. **The database is the only home for hotel data** — nothing is kept in the browser (the owner's ruling, 2026-09-28).

## Ownership

- Primary Owner: Frontend Engineers / Antigravity Agent
- Scope: `src/utils/**` (except the restaurant tab modules, which belong to the restaurant doc).

    - [dailyReport.ts](file:///c:/Users/dev4s/Documents/Programming/Daweez-booking/src/utils/dailyReport.ts) — **the Daily Report's figures** (the owner's design, 2026-09-29): one day's money in and money out, and nothing else. His four rulings ARE the specification — *"this is supposed to be a report of how much money was received and spent, not promised"* (so it counts **dated receipts**, never a booking's undated `downpayment_paid`), *"this report is for money only. nothing else"* (no check-ins, no occupancy), *"can we just list where the money came from? and not summarize it?"* (one line per receipt and per expense, named `Room 4 · SEB · Cash` — no category buckets, no Cash/GCash sub-totals), and the conclusion last. `buildDailyReport({ date, bookings, tabs, expenses, categories, rooms })` is **pure**, so the screen and the printed sheet cannot disagree, and it returns `moneyIn`/`moneyOut` lines plus `totalIn`/`totalOut`/`net`. Money in comes from `payment_records` on **bookings and tabs** (each record carries the `paid_at` the money arrived and its own method) **grouped by receipt number**, so **one payment is one line, naming every room it covered** (`SEB · Rooms 4–10 · Cash`) — a payment is now stored on every room it paid for (2026-09-30), and each of those rows carries the **whole** amount, so listing the rows as they come would print ₱9,200 seven times. The grouping is what makes the sheet say the money was taken **once**; the `undatedMoney` guard compares a room's holding against the **share** it can claim rather than the whole payment, or every multi-room booking would read as overpaid; **`hotelDay(iso)` is the one place the hotel's calendar day is decided** — built from LOCAL parts, never `.toISOString()` (UTC would move every pre-8 AM payment in UTC+8 onto yesterday's sheet, which is exactly when the desk takes money), and **it is also where the cut-off hour goes** if the owner asks for one, since SEB's ₱1,900 arrived at 2:45 AM. Two guards matter: `payment_records` is **JSON `null` on most rows** (never assume an array — `jsonb_array_elements` on it is how a query fails with *"cannot extract elements from a scalar"*), and money a legacy booking holds with **no dated receipt** is returned as `undatedMoney`/`undatedCount` rather than dropped, because a money report must never lose money in silence.

## Work Guidance

- **Breakfast (cards k140/k142)**: Breakfast is the **ROOM's** choice, never a guest's, and it is **one charge for the whole stay at that room's own breakfast price** — a price the desk types per room in Settings, blank until they do. It is **one line for the whole stay**, `Room 6 · Breakfast`, printed in the statement's own **`Breakfast:` band** (the owner's 2026-09 table) — never per guest and never per day, and never folded into the room row. The per-day `BreakfastRecorder` is **retired and not rendered**; the ₱150 × beds idea was dropped when the owner asked for a price per room instead, so **no bed count is used anywhere**. Legacy `breakfast_records` (per-day, per-person) still price old bookings, and the bill prints those rows for a venue booking only. **`calculatePricing` charges it ONLY when it is told — `breakfastIncluded: true` — so every re-pricer must hand that flag over from the booking.** `createManualBooking` (`hooks/useBookings.ts`) did not, and stored every new booking owing the **room alone** while ticking `breakfast_included` on the row: GRF-2026-09-0014 held ₱1,800 against a ₱2,400 stay, so the printed bill showed Sub-Total ₱2,400 with an **Amount Due of ₱1,800** copied from that short balance — the same failure shape as the short-stay rule above, where a re-pricer not handed the flag prices a different stay than the one stored. `recomputeBalance`, `bookingPricing` and the Earnings report all pass it correctly, which is why the ₱600 reappeared the moment the guest was checked in. **The ₱150-per-head breakfast rule is DELETED — every trace of it** (the owner's ruling, 2026-09-29: *"if a room has no breakfast price, then breakfast should be unavailable. remove every trace of 150 per head for breakfast calculations since the staff decides what the breakfast price is"*). What that removed, and why it mattered: `calculatePricing` carried **two fallbacks that invented a charge** — one billed `rates.breakfastPrice × guests × nights` to **any room the desk had not priced yet**, and one billed `rates.breakfastPrice × quantity` for a legacy `breakfast_order` (a `BreakfastOrder` carries no price of its own to bill). The first is what charged **Room 4 ₱300 for two nights the guest was never offered**: the booking form quoted ₱1,900, the engine stored ₱2,200, and the receipt handed to the guest read **₱300 remaining** on a stay they had paid in full. Gone with it: `rates.breakfastPrice` (there is **no** breakfast figure in `RateConfig` at all now), the `breakfastGuestCount` / `breakfastDays` / `breakfastOrders` / `companions` inputs to `calculatePricing`, and the printed `Breakfast (₱150/guest/night)` row `statementLines` built by dividing the total back down by ₱150. **The only breakfast prices are the ones the staff typed**: the room's own `breakfast_price` (one charge for the stay), and the price stored on each legacy `breakfast_record` when it was served. The guest portal already quotes the **room's** figure, so portal, form, engine and printed bill now agree by construction — a room with no breakfast price sells none on every surface.

- **Short stays (the printed rate board)**: `calculatePricing` takes `shortStayHours` (3/6/12/22 — 22 is still accepted because a booking saved while the form still offered it must keep pricing correctly, but nothing sells it any more: 22 hours IS the room's normal price, so it is an ordinary overnight booking booked from a date range). A short stay is **ONE charge**, never nights × rate: the price is the room's own figure for those hours (`hour3_price` / `hour6_price` / `hour12_price`), and for 22 hours it is the room's normal single price — that 22-hour column **is** the price the app has always charged. `stayQuantity` is 1 and `stayUnit` becomes `3 HOURS`, so the bill and the statement print hours instead of nights. **Every reader that re-prices a booking MUST pass `shortStayHours: <booking>.stay_hours`** — `recomputeBalance` (`bookingBalance.ts`), `bookingPricing` (`statement.ts`), the Earnings report (`useAnalyticsCalculations.ts`) and `createManualBooking` (`useBookings.ts`). Those four skipped it at first, so a 3-hour stay on Room 8 was ₱550 in the booking form and **₱850 — a whole night —** in the quick view, at check-out (which then WROTE ₱850 into the booking), on the printed bill and in the Earnings report (the owner's S1 ruling, 2026-09). Only the two `calculatePricing` calls that price an **extension** (`CalendarTab` / `ExtendStayModal`) leave it out on purpose: a stay extended past its day becomes a normal night. `chargeableEarlyHours` returns **0** whenever `stay_hours` is set, and `ExtendStayModal` skips the early/late computation for it: a three-hour guest arriving at 10am is not four hours early for a 2pm check-in, and without that gate they would be charged a whole extra night at check-out.

- **Null-Safe Pricing Reads**: a database round-trip turns a missing array into `null`, so `calculatePricing` normalizes what it reads (`const brkRecords = breakfastRecords || []`) and every caller handing stored booking fields to the invoice must do the same (`PrintInvoiceModal` maps `null` → `[]`) so a re-priced invoice never crashes or invents a charge.

- **Local Date Strings (timezone-safe)**: Booking dates (`check_in`/`check_out`) are plain `YYYY-MM-DD` calendar-day strings. Always build them with `dateToString(date)` from `utils/helpers.ts` (local components) — never `.toISOString().split('T')[0]`, which shifts dates back one day in UTC+8. This applies to calendar cell keys, booking creation/confirmation, "today" comparisons (briefing, dashboard stats, bookings list), and date-input defaults/minimums.

- **No browser storage for hotel data (the owner's ruling, 2026-09-28)**: the database is the **only** home for every price, booking, tab, expense, agency, menu, inventory and cleaning record, and for the shared rates and where-guests-pay. Code must still check `isSupabaseConfigured`, but the two branches now differ only in what they report: with no backend the reads return empty and every write **throws** so the screen says the save failed. There is **no offline store** — a write that cannot reach the database is refused out loud, never parked in the browser. **The one exception is the staff login** (`daweez_pms_auth` in `localStorage`), which the owner ruled stays: it is a session, not hotel data. This replaced a fallback that made a refused save look successful — which is how the hotel's room prices were typed, shown on every screen and never written.

- **Background Calendar Sync**: Third-party bookings (Airbnb/Booking.com) must sync automatically via iCal feeds in the background upon mounting the application and at regular intervals (5 minutes) thereafter, with a manual OTA Sync trigger as a backup. **The switches are the owner's to throw** (2026-09-30): Settings → Channels carries a master on/off for the whole connection and one switch per room, stored in `app_settings` key `channel_sync` (`utils/channelSync.ts`). **A missing setting, or anything that is not an explicit `false`, means ON** — a half-written setting must never leave the hotel silently un-synced. **The server enforces it**, not the browser: the `sync-ical` edge function reads the same row before it fetches anything, so an old tab on another machine cannot sync a room the desk switched off. A room that is off is **left completely alone** — not fetched *and not tidied*, since the same run deletes sync rows the feed no longer lists — and **the expired-website-hold cleanup deliberately is NOT behind the master switch**, because that is the app's own job and switching the channels off must not leave abandoned holds blocking rooms. **And ONE ADDRESS LIVES ON ONE ROOM** (`utils/feedUrls.ts`, the owner's ruling, 2026-09-30: *"do what you think is good"*). A feed is a calendar, and a calendar belongs to a listing: put one URL on nine rooms and the sync fetches that file nine times and imports **every reservation in it once per room** — which is how one Airbnb stay came to block **nine rooms** for the same night, why Analytics and Expenses waited ~7 seconds on the shared load, and what the owner found as "20 feeds, 2 addresses, 11 of them blank". `findFeedUrlClashes` is the rule; `ChannelFeeds` refuses to save a clash and names the room that already holds the address, so the mistake cannot be typed twice. **A feed with a blank address is not a row at all** — `db.saveFeeds` stores only filled links and **deletes** a row whose box was cleared, because the Channels screen draws two empty boxes per room and saving those placeholders wrote 20 rows for a hotel with one real calendar.
