What that changes

A price you control

A room can be ₱900, ₱950, or ₱0 (no breakfast sold) — per room, set once.

Beds become a suggestion

The bed count only offers the starting figure (6 × ₱150 = ₱900). It stops being the rule.

Nothing else moves
