Normal booking is the road — the agency is a small side door

You are right: an agency is rare, so it must not stand beside the normal booking as an equal. The booking door opens straight into the normal process, every time. The agency is one small door off it, off to the side.

Thick line = the road every booking takes. Thin dashed = the small door off it, which is the only path that walks steps before it can confirm.

What it means in the app

· Picking dates and confirming always lands in the normal booking form — unchanged, first, and the only thing the staff see by default.
· One quiet line sits in that form, near the top, that says “Booking for an agency?” — small, not a button of equal weight. Tapping it opens the agency steps.
· The calendar bar keeps only New booking (the separate Corporate button goes — no second door of equal size).
· Only the agency path walks steps and prints the COMPANY line on the bill.
