The receive step at 323px — three ways to fix it

Your screenshot is the panel at its narrowest (323px). Three things break at that width, all of them because the row is asked to hold two things side by side:

1. the methods wrap: Cash and GCash on one line, Bank transfer dropped to its own ragged line.  2. the reference row squeezes a fixed 180px box past the edge, so the label and the box fight.  3. the gold button is left with ~170px, so “Record ₱625 received” breaks into two lines beside Cancel.

What is on screen vs what I drew

You are right, they are not the same — and the whole difference is one press. The drawing was the state AFTER pressing. On screen the money block shows one button first — Guest has paid — and the method list, the reference and Record ₱625 received appear when it is pressed. That is the “one next step at a time, nothing until it is needed” rule you asked for; my drawing just showed the opened step, so it looked like it should all be there at once.

Wording — changed as asked

The buttons only write it down — the money is already in hand

Both labels promised something that already happened. The desk takes the cash or reads the GCash message first, then presses; nothing about the press is what receives the money.

The button that opens the step

Receive money → Guest has paid

The button that records it

Receive ₱625 & print receipt → Record ₱625 received

“ & print receipt” is gone from the label. The receipt still comes out — it always has, straight after the payment is recorded — it just is not promised on the button, because the press is not what receives the money and the paper is not what the press is for.

Confirm the words: “Guest has paid” for the opener and “Record ₱625 received” for the confirm — or say the wording you want and I will use yours.

What every option keeps

Cash · GCash · Bank transfer only. No tick, no circle, no emoji — the chosen method is gold and bold, nothing else. No Save button: tapping writes it down. Reference required for GCash and bank, none for cash. The amount is fixed (₱625 — the rest of the bill), never typed. One loud action, and it only writes down money the staff have already taken; the receipt prints after that.
