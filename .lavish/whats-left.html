A guest asks to see what they have run up so far. Next to the running total on the Guest tab there is now a quiet Print the tab button, and out comes this — on the same roll as everything else:

Settles with the room bill at check-out.

It is not a receipt, and it says so — that was the point of stamping NOT A RECEIPT across it. No money has changed hands, so the paper must never look like one.

The last line says what happens next: on a stay "Settles with the room bill at check-out.", and on a walk-in tab "Please settle this at the counter."

Both kinds of tab can print it — the stay's tab in the booking quick view, and the walk-in's tab in the Restaurant screen.

E · Restaurant money in the Earnings Report

built ✓

Food money had nowhere to go in the report — it would have landed in the room column or gone missing. The restaurant is now its own line, and it counts in the totals.

Before

Pension ₱12,000 · Vacation House ₱4,000 · Garden ₱1,500 · Gazebo ₱1,500

Food and drinks ordered on tabs: nowhere

Total Money In

₱19,000

Now

Pension ₱12,000 · Vacation House ₱4,000 · Garden ₱1,500 · Gazebo ₱1,500

Restaurant & bar ₱3,450

Total Money In

₱22,450

Counted on the day the food was ordered, the same way a night is counted on the night it was slept — so a ₱220 lunch shows up on the day it was eaten, not spread over the stay.

It follows the whole report: its own row and column in the Earnings Report, its own card in the visual view, a slice in the donut, and a gold bar in Income Over Time.

Both kinds of tab count — food on a stay's bill and a walk-in diner's lunch.

One honest limit: it counts what was ordered in the period, like the report already does for rooms — not what has been paid for yet.
