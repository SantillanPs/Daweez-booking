“if some information earned 0 income, don’t include it in the print, also, remove some redundancies”

Your rule, written down

  1. A line prints only if it carries money. No Restaurant & bar ₱0, no GCash ₱0, no Bank transfer ₱0.
  2. A heading prints only if something sits under it. No income means no MONEY TAKEN TODAY; no expenses means no expense block at all.
  3. No total that just repeats the only line above it. One room payment of ₱1,900 does not also need a TOTAL IN ₱1,900.
  4. The method list prints only when money came in more than one way. All-cash means the method adds nothing — the line already says Cash.
  5. NET only when there is something to net. With no expenses, NET ₱1,900 is the same figure a third time.
