Your question

The Bookings tab — what it does, and what it does not

What it does today

It is a list of every booking with two jobs done well:

· Find a guest fast — by name, invoice number, the paper log number, or the room.

· Say who owes money — a headline figure at the top, and one-tap filters for Who owes and Paid, with a plain badge on each row.

Each row can print the bill or open details. Upcoming stays sort first.

What it does not do — and this is the honest problem

It cannot do anything. You cannot check a guest in, check them out, or receive money from it. It is a read-only list: every real action still sends you back to the calendar, opening one booking at a time.

So today it solves “where is this guest and what do they owe” — not “run my front desk”.
