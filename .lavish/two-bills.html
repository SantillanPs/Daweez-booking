The six differences

1 · The charges table
  • Paper: one row per room — Room / Rate / Check In / Check Out / No. of Night / Total, under a “Room Accommodation:” band.
  • Ours: Description / Qty / Unit / Price / Discount / Amount.
2 · The money line
  • Paper prints TOTAL only — no Sub-Total, no Deposit, no Amount Due.
  • Ours prints Sub-Total → less deposit → Amount Due.
3 · The bottom of the page
  • Paper: Prepared by only — no Pension Policies and no Guest Signature.
  • Ours prints the policies verbatim and both signature lines, on every bill.
4 · Ways to pay
  • Paper adds Check and lists BOTH bank accounts — Landbank (Jonathan E. Dango) and BPI — plus GCash.
  • Ours holds one bank account and has no Check; it prints only the method chosen.
5 · The guest block
  • Paper: Company · Name of Guest (c/o) · Address · Email · Contact No. · Vehicle Plate #.
  • Ours adds Nationality, Birth Date, Sex, No. of Guests and prints TIN instead of the plate.
6 · The last line
  • Paper ends with “THANK YOU FOR CHOOSING DAWEEZ PENSION HOUSE”.
  • Ours ends with the signing sentence.
