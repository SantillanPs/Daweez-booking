You are right — and that is the argument for the switch

The employee of the agency is doing exactly what a normal booking does: picking rooms, dates, a guest. So the form should not be replaced or split — the only new question is “who is this bill for?”. One switch at the top answers it, and everything else stays where the desk already knows it.

Why the switch, and not a second form

Nothing is relearnedThe desk books the rooms the same way, in the same order. Only one extra choice sits at the top, and it is already the thing the desk knows before touching anything: is this guest paying, or an agency?
Nothing appears until neededA guest booking looks exactly like today — the agency fields do not exist on screen. Tap Agency and they appear, filled from the agency's profile, editable for a one-off.
The bill can't be addressed wrongThe addressee is decided before the rooms and the money are read, not discovered at the bottom of the form. Today's Corporate form is the opposite — it starts by picking the agency and then books on its behalf, which is the “automatic booking” your note said no to.
