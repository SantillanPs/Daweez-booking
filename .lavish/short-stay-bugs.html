Closing the receipt closes the booking panel — and with it, the Check in

For a short stay the clock only starts when you press Check in. Today, recording the first money prints the receipt, and closing that receipt closes the panel — so the check-in step disappears with it and you have to find the booking again.

PROPOSED
Closing the receipt leaves the panel open while the guest has not been checked in yetthe Check in is still there
For an ordinary booking, where nothing is left to do, it closes as it does todayunchanged
