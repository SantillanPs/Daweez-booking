What is different on every tile:

- **The food group is an icon** — a coffee cup for Breakfast, a beer mug for Beer — so the group is found without reading it.
- **The price is now the loudest thing on the tile**: big, gold, Sora. The price is what gets said out loud at the counter.
- **A dish already ordered lights up with a count** (`Cornsilog ×2`), so the same order cannot land twice unnoticed.
- The long choice notes came off the face — they moved to the tile's tooltip, where they are still there when needed.

Built for your staff at the counter, not for a guest holding the tablet: bigger targets, less prose, more cue.

I kept the hotel's charcoal-and-gold palette and got the contrast from icon, size and the count rather than fifteen different colours — the project's brand rules say gold and charcoal only. If you want a colour per food group on top of that, say so and I will add it; it is your palette.
