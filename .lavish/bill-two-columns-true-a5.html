Where the space comes from

Nothing is made smaller. The same lines simply stop taking a whole row each.

Three row-pairs do the work: the letterhead (hotel details left, bill number right — it stacks today only because the two-column breakpoint needs a wider screen than A5 has), Guest beside Stay (14 labelled lines become 7 rows), and Payment Method beside Totals (the tick boxes were wasting the full width of the sheet).
