Removed — the **Short stay** row is gone from the booking form, both in a normal booking and in a short-stay one. The form no longer asks about hours at all.

Where the hours come from now: **the calendar's action bar**, where you tap `3h · 6h · 12h` on the day. The form opens with them already settled, and states them where the deposit used to be: **`3-hour stay · ₱550 — paid in full at the counter`**.

Two things follow from it, so you know:

- **22 hours is no longer sellable anywhere.** It was only in that row, and 22 hours is the room's own price — an ordinary overnight booking from a date range. (The pricing rule still accepts it, so an older booking saved with 22 keeps its price.)
- The picker file is deleted, not just hidden.
