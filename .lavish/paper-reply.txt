Narrowed — day columns **96 → 84 px**, which trims about an eighth off the grid's width without touching the block.

84 is the useful floor: `SEBASTIAN` and `out 8:02 AM` both still fit on their two lines at that width. Go narrower and the name starts cutting again — so if the columns still feel too wide, the better lever is the block's own wording rather than the column.
