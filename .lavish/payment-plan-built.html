The payment plan is in the booking form

Deposit is still the standard. Full pay and Custom sit beside it. Nothing is asked for unless the desk taps it.

Where you will see it

Calendar → pick a day (or a range) → New booking → scroll to WHAT THE GUEST PAYS NOW. One line, three states:

1 · DEPOSIT — the default, nothing to tap
What the guest pays now
DepositFull payCustom ₱1,250

2 nights · Room ₱2,500 — whole stay ₱2,500. Half is ₱1,250; the rest is collected at check-out.

Read-only, exactly as you said. Just the figure.

2 · FULL PAY — for the guest who pays it all
What the guest pays now
DepositFull payCustom ₱2,500

2 nights · Room ₱2,500 — whole stay ₱2,500. Paid in full now — nothing left at check-out.

Read-only too. One tap, and the whole stay is what is asked for.

3 · CUSTOM — the only one you type in
What the guest pays now
DepositFull payCustom 1,000 · ₱1,500 after

2 nights · Room ₱2,500 — whole stay ₱2,500. The rest is owed with the stay.

Here the second number is a different number, so it stays beside it.

4 · SHORT STAY — untouched

3-hour stay · ₱550 — paid in full at the counter

No deposit and no statement: the guest pays the whole amount now.

A 3/6/12-hour stay still skips the plan line entirely.

What is behind it

PlanThe figureSaved with the booking
Deposithalf the stay, read-onlypayment_plan = deposit + that figure
Full paythe whole stay, read-onlypayment_plan = full + that figure
Customwhat you typepayment_plan = custom + what you typed

The printed bill names it: Deposit (50%) · Full payment · Custom · ₱1,000 now — a bare “Custom” would tell the guest nothing.

Re-opening a booking for an edit puts the plan and the typed figure back in the line, so saving never blanks them.

What did not change

· The form still takes no money — the guest gets the statement, and you record the payment from the booking's quick view, which prints their receipt.
· Deposit stays the default on every new booking.
· Deposit and Full pay can’t be typed into — the figures are the app’s.
