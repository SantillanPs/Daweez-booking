Two things still waiting on you

1. B2 is solved fixed

Your two answers were the clue. Both saving functions do keep the name, and 6 bookings prove it can be stored — so the name was being wiped afterwards, not lost on the way in.

The culprit was the live-updates hook that keeps the calendar in step. It rebuilds each booking from the database row, and its list of columns had fallen behind — prepared_by was missing. So the moment a booking arrived or changed over live updates, the name disappeared from the screen, and the very next save (recording a payment, checking a guest in) wrote the booking back without it. The same gap was quietly wiping the check-in and check-out times, the recorded payments, the staff discount, the block reason, the early and late hours, the gazebo day-blocks, the planned breakfast days and the guest's birth date.

They are all carried now, and the rule is written into the project's own instructions so a new column can never be forgotten again.
