What has to change

Four things, all in one place — the booking form.

01

The form gains a payment method

Cash · GCash · Bank transfer · Check — the same four the quick view already offers, so there is one list in the app, not two. Nothing preselected: an empty box, exactly as the quick view insists (Choose…, never a silent Cash).

02

GCash and Bank ask for the reference

The reference box appears only for those two, and the form will not finish until it is filled — which is the whole point of your step 5. Cash and Check have no reference, so they finish straight away.

03

Confirm means the money is in

Pressing Confirm now records the payment as well as creating the booking — so the booking arrives already confirmed by its own money, instead of sitting Unpaid / On hold until somebody records it later.

04

The panel shows the receipt

After finishing, the paper on screen is the Payment Receipt — not the billing statement. The statement is still printable any time from the booking's quick view.

This reverses a ruling on purpose. The form was deliberately built to take no money and ask for no method (card k132). Nothing here is a bug — it is a decision you are changing, so the docs will be rewritten rather than patched.

03

The one thing that gets tight

Your own rule: the Pays now row costs one line of height, never a row per option. Adding a method and a reference has to grow it.

04

The decision to make first

When Confirm Booking is pressed, what finishes the form?

A Reservation is the odd one out: it pays nothing by definition, so it can never satisfy "wait until the money is in".
