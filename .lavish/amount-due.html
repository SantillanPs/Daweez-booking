Why Amount Due is ₱1,800 on a ₱2,400 stay

The paper and the saved booking were worked out in two different places, and one of them was never told the room has breakfast. The gap is exactly the breakfast: ₱600.

the paper asks the SAVED booking what the stay costs

Sub-Total₱2,400
Amount Due ← the odd one₱1,800

The paper knows the stay is ₱2,400 — and then asks for ₱1,800, because the “Amount Due” figure is not worked out from the Sub-Total above it. It is copied from the booking's stored balance.

the booking as saved what was written when it was made

Room 6, one night₱1,800
Breakfast, once for the stay₱0
balance_due₱1,800

Breakfast is ticked on the booking (breakfast_included: true) — but the price it was saved with was worked out before that flag was handed to the pricing engine.

The cause, in one line

When a booking is saved, the app asks the price engine for the figures and forgets to say “this room has breakfast” — so the saved balance is the room alone. Every other reader (the printed bill, the badge after check-in, the Earnings report) does pass that flag, which is why the paper and the balance disagree by exactly the breakfast.

src/hooks/useBookings.ts → createManualBooking → calculatePricing({ … }) — breakfastIncluded is missing

What it does at the desk — three ways

1. The booking badge reads Owes ₱1,800 for a ₱2,400 stay, and the check-in gate uses that same number — so ₱1,800 can be taken at the desk and the booking still counts as settled for entry.
2. The moment the guest is checked in, the app re-prices the stay with breakfast — and ₱600 quietly appears as owed again. That is the “where did this come from?” moment.
3. A Deposit booking loses half of it too (half of ₱1,800 instead of half of ₱2,400), and the guest portal's copy of the same rule is priced the old way (₱150 × guests × days) rather than the room's own figure.

The fix, drawn — one flag, one line

before

The badge, the gate and the paper all read ₱1,800.

after

The saved balance carries the breakfast, so every screen, the badge and the paper agree with the Sub-Total.

Separate flag — your room prices are not in the database

The database says blank for every breakfast price and every short-stay price — the same thing that happened with Room 8's 3h/6h/12h figures.
