Three things I'll treat as settled unless you say otherwise

All three follow from what you've already told me — I'd rather say them out loud than ask three more questions.

1 · The amount recorded is the agreed figure. Deposit records half, Full pay records the whole stay, and Custom is how the desk types anything else — so there is no second amount box to fill in and the block keeps its size.

2 · A group booking records the money once. Several rooms under one invoice, one payment on the invoice — the receipt then covers them as a set, the way the statement already does.

3 · Nothing already saved is touched. This changes what a new booking does. Existing bookings keep the money they hold, and the quick view remains the way to add to them.
