Daweez · card k144 · organising the information · nothing built yet

It's not badly ordered — it's unlabelled.

Right now eight lines sit in two columns with nothing saying which belong together: the guest's name is beside a room number, a guest's contact number is beneath a check-out date, and there is no heading anywhere. A reader has to work out for themselves which line is about who is staying and which is about the stay. Below: the same fields grouped under headings, three ways.

03

Two details that come with it

The heading style. All three mocks use a light band behind the word. I could equally use a plain underlined word, or a thin rule across the sheet — tell me if you'd rather see that. And the field order inside each section is fixed: your sparse booking and the full booking put the same fields in the same place, so a bill always reads the same way.
