The stacking is the real find. Between the last field row and the first table row there are two py-3 paddings meeting — 24px of air for a boundary that needs 8. The same happens again below the table. Set the blocks to py-2 and give the table no vertical padding of its own, and the page loses ~30px while looking more deliberate, because the spacing is then owned by one thing instead of three.
