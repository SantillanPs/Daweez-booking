02

One thing worth knowing

This block only prints while no method has been chosen. Once the desk records how the guest paid, the page shows just that one method and its Account Details instead — the full bank name, unshortened, because that is the copy a guest reads while making the transfer. So whatever we pick here is the fallback, seen on a booking nobody has paid yet.
