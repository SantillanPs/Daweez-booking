Today is the first column, in the gold circle. Nothing before it is drawn at all — there is nothing to scroll back through.

1 · Today first

The window begins at today's date while you are on the current month. Sep 20 opens at Sep 20, not Sep 1.

2 · Always 31 days wide

Late in a month it keeps going into the days ahead instead of squashing a handful of columns across the screen. 1 Oct is written in the header so the month change is obvious.

3 · A Today button

The old “This month” button is now Today — it brings you back from any month, and a small from today badge sits by the title while the grid really does start today.

4 · A stay that started earlier

Any guest already in the house shows a small ‹ before their name, so a cut-off block is never read as a fresh arrival. Hovering still tells you the real arrival date.

What you see when you open Calendar today

Sep 20 – Oct 20, 2026 from today ‹ Sep 2026-09 Oct › Today
Month you navigate to (e.g. Nov, or Aug)opens on its 1st, as before
Current monthopens on today
Scrolling sideways, then changing monthsnaps back to the left edge

Bookings, blocking, group selection, New booking, Corporate and Log old booking all work exactly as they did — only the set of day columns changed.

Check it in 10 seconds

1Open the Calendar tab.
2The first column must be today, 20 Sep, in the gold circle.
3Press Oct › then Today — you come straight back to 20 Sep at the left edge.

Type-check passes (npx tsc -b --force → exit 0). If the tab was already open, reload the page once.

One thing to decide

A minute before midnight on the 30th you would see 31 columns: one day of September and then all of October. Is that right, or would you rather the grid always stop at the last day of the month and just leave the rest of the screen empty?

A · What it does now (my pick)

Never shrinks, always 31 days of “what's coming”, month change marked in the header.

B · Stop at the month end

Late in the month the grid is shorter and the right side is empty. Booking over a month end needs the Oct › button first.
