02

Reservations — what I understand you to mean

A hold for somebody the staff personally know and trust. No payment, no plan, no system check on who they are — the desk decides, because they would not reserve for a stranger.

The one real conflict: a reserved guest cannot be checked in today

The app refuses check-in while money is owed — the Check in button is not even drawn — because that is where the balance is normally collected. A reservation is unpaid by design, so without opening that door the guest could never be checked in at all, and the feature would do nothing. This is the question below.
