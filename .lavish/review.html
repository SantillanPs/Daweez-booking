The stock room is built.

Stock roomItems with a unit, a price, a par level, and what the shelf is worth. Receive on each row. In the Housekeeping tab.
What a dish usesOpen a dish, add its stock lines. A drink is one line, a cooked dish four or five. The screen counts the dishes that still need them.
AutomaticTap a dish at the till → the shelf goes down by itself. Take the line off → every gram goes back.
The logEvery movement: what, how many, in or out, why, who. One sale writes several lines.
The columnThe sold line now keeps the dish's id — the one thing that made all of this possible.
