Where it broke

1Booking made — saved at the single price, ₱2,850.
2Deposit recorded, then the rest — ₱2,850 received. Both receipts saved fine.
3Opening the booking (and checking in) works the bill out again from the room's price.
4Nothing on the booking said “this one is at the single price” — so it picked the old regular ₱3,800.
5Result: ₱2,850 of ₱3,800 · ₱950 to receive — and the printed bill would have said ₱3,800 too.

The money was never lost: both payments were there, with their receipts. Only the price it was compared against was wrong.
