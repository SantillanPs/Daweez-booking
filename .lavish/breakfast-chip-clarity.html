The three rules behind it

✓Tick = breakfast on that room. Empty circle + dashed edge = off. No colour to learn.
₱The line under it repeats it in words — “Breakfast added: ₱450 once for the stay · Room 6”, or “No breakfast on this booking.”
—A room with no breakfast price can no longer be tapped. It used to look ticked and quietly charge ₱0. It now says no price and the line tells you to set it in Settings → Room Rates.
