P2 · your pick

Everyone open, always visible: bill left, till right

No cards and no switch. One strip across the top lists everyone you are serving — every guest who is in the hotel, plus every diner tab — each with their own total, so switching tables is one tap. The selected bill is a running receipt on the left, and the till takes the rest as one long menu, separated by category: the staff just scroll, the way they read the paper card.
