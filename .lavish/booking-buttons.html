Buttons that pop up at the top — five designs

Same rule in all five: nothing appears until you pick dates, and what appears depends on what you picked — one day gives Short stay, a range gives New booking and Corporate.

built — design 1, then fixed three times

Why Short stay never appeared

A picked day draws itself as the gold “In” cell — and that cell was the one cell in the grid with no position markers on it, so the bar could not find where to hang. Single day = the Short stay offer, which is exactly when nothing showed. Fixed: that cell carries the markers now, so the button appears.

“I chose room 6 and the buttons are far from the date and room”the bar is pinned to that cell, not the top of the grid
“the buttons are popping up below the dates”moved up onto the picked row — then one step higher again, see below
settled

It sits just above the picked row

Your catch: on the row, it covered the very dates you had just chosen. It now floats one bar height above the picked row — plus a small gap so it does not read as touching the row above either — so the room and its dates stay visible. For the top rows, where the sticky day header is, it drops below the row instead, because that is the only room left on screen.

“move the buttons 2 columns to the left”its left edge now starts two day columns before the picked day
“move the buttons a tiny bit more up”gap above the row widened to 18px
“instead of the Short stay button, show the 3 options — 3, 6 and 12 hours”one day now offers 3h · 6h · 12h with the room's own prices, and tapping one opens the form with those hours already chosen
